Hello World

March 30, 2025

It’s been a while since I felt the need to express my thoughts, questions, struggles, and findings.

I’ve tried to adapt to social media formats, but I always end up feeling trapped by all the constraints that come with them. Even though I don’t care about views—and I actively dislike most of the enforced formats that dominate social platforms—adapting to the environment seems inevitable.

We are only human, after all. That’s what we do: we adapt.

Even with my personal blog, I sometimes feel constrained. Take the tool I use to generate these pages—it’s flexible, sure, but still a cage in some ways. The difference? This time, it’s a cage for which I own the key. And I will use it. I will open it.

I’m excited for what comes next.
